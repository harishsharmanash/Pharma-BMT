# TICKET H2 — Row-spacing pass so three dense control pairs can take 44px hit areas

## Background — why these three were deliberately skipped

The app has a `.hit-area-44` utility in `src/styles.css` (`::after` with `inset: -8px`) that expands
a control's *hit* area without changing its visible size. It is applied to nine controls already.

**Three places were deliberately left out**, because of the adjacency rule:

> Where two icon buttons sit adjacent with a small gap, applying `.hit-area-44` to BOTH makes their
> invisible areas overlap. The later element wins in the overlap and partially swallows the first —
> which is worse than the original small target.

So those three need their **spacing** increased first. That is this ticket. Do the spacing only —
**do NOT add `.hit-area-44` anywhere in this ticket.** A follow-up will add it once the gaps are
verified on a real device.

## The three sites

1. **`src/components/staff/claims-tab.tsx`** — Approve (`Check`, ~line 82) and Reject (`X`, ~line 88)
   sit adjacent in a table row's action cell. These are **destructive-ish, irreversible decisions on
   money**; a mis-tap approves an expense claim. Spacing matters most here.
2. **`src/components/staff/attendance-tab.tsx`** — Previous month (`ChevronLeft`, ~line 91) and Next
   month (`ChevronRight`, ~line 98) flank a month label.
3. **`src/routes/products.stock.tsx`** — the `TabsTrigger` pills (~lines 58-61: Overview, Batches,
   Inward / Purchases, Issue & Returns) sit in a dense row.

## What to do

- Increase the **gap between the adjacent controls** so each can later carry a 44px hit area without
  the areas overlapping. In practice that means a gap of at least `gap-2` (8px) and ideally `gap-3`
  (12px) between the two icon buttons, achieved on the *container*, not by adding margins to each
  button.
- Where the controls sit in a table action cell, make sure the cell itself is not so tight that
  increasing the gap causes wrapping or column overflow. Keep the row height stable.
- For the stock tabs, ensure the trigger row can breathe: adequate gap between pills, and the strip
  must remain horizontally scrollable on narrow viewports (wrap it in `overflow-x-auto` if it is not
  already) rather than wrapping to two lines or clipping.
- Keep every visible size, colour, label, icon and handler exactly as-is. **Spacing only.**

## Hard rules
- **Do not add `.hit-area-44`** to any of these controls in this ticket.
- Do not change any `onClick`, mutation, permission gate, or `aria-label`.
- Do not touch `src/components/ui/*` — a change there ripples app-wide, which is precisely why this
  problem was solved with a call-site utility instead of resizing the shared Button.
- Do not restyle anything beyond the spacing needed.
