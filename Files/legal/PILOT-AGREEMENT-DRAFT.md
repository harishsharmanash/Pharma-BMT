2. **`[Client legal name]`**, `[address]`, GSTIN `[ ]` (**"the Client"**).

## 1. What this agreement covers

Cerebyl gives the Client access to the Cerebyl business-management application at
`https://app.cerebyl.com` (and any Cerebyl-branded or Client-branded Android app built for the
Client), including CRM, orders and invoicing, products, stock, team, the distributor portal, the
Ceremate AI assistant and, where the Client connects it, WhatsApp messaging (together, **"the
Service"**).

## 2. Pilot period

The pilot runs from `[start date]` for **`[3]` months** and continues month to month afterwards
until either party ends it under clause 9, or both parties sign a paid subscription agreement.

## 3. What is free, and what is charged

3.1 **No subscription fee** is charged for the Service during the pilot.

3.2 **AI usage is charged** at the rates shown in the app's AI usage screen at the time of use,
currently:
- Ceremate assistant: `[₹ per message / ₹ per image / ₹ per PDF — copy from ai_limits]`;
- WhatsApp auto-reply bot: **₹0.50 per bot reply** (one charge per reply, however many message
  bubbles it is split into).

3.3 **WhatsApp conversation charges set by Meta** are billed by Meta to the payment method on the
Client's own WhatsApp Business account, not by Cerebyl. `[Confirm this matches how each client's
number was onboarded.]`

3.4 Cerebyl may change AI rates with **`[30]` days' written notice** (email is enough). The Client
can set a spending cap for the WhatsApp bot in the app; Cerebyl will not bill beyond it.

## 4. Invoicing and payment

Cerebyl will send a monthly statement of AI usage and an invoice, with GST where applicable, within
`[7]` days after month end. Payment is due within `[15]` days of the invoice. If an invoice is
unpaid `[30]` days after its due date, Cerebyl may pause AI features until it is paid; the rest of
the Service keeps working and no data is withheld.

## 5. The Client's data

5.1 The Client owns all data it enters. For personal data of the Client's own customers,
distributors, leads and staff, **the Client is the Data Fiduciary and Cerebyl is its Data
Processor** under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. The **Data Processing Agreement**
published at `https://app.cerebyl.com/legal/dpa` forms part of this agreement.

5.2 Data is stored in India (Mumbai). The sub-processors are listed in the DPA.

5.3 Cerebyl does not sell the Client's data and does not use it to train AI models.

5.4 The Client is responsible for having a lawful basis to message its contacts on WhatsApp and for
honouring opt-outs; the Service records STOP requests and excludes those numbers from broadcasts.

## 6. The pilot is provided as-is

The Service is under active development. Cerebyl will use reasonable care and skill and will fix
reported defects promptly, but does not guarantee uninterrupted or error-free operation during the
pilot. AI output can be wrong and must be checked before it is relied on — in particular rupee
figures, dues and stock numbers. **Cerebyl is a business tool and makes no medical claims.**

## 7. Liability

Each party's total liability under this agreement is limited to the AI usage fees paid by the Client
in the **`[3]` months** before the event giving rise to the claim. Neither party is liable for
indirect or consequential loss, including lost profits. Nothing limits liability that cannot be
limited by law.

## 8. Confidentiality

Each party keeps the other's non-public business information confidential and uses it only for this
agreement. This survives termination for `[2]` years.

## 9. Ending the pilot

Either party may end this agreement with **`[15]` days' written notice**. On request made within
`[30]` days after it ends, Cerebyl will provide an export of the Client's data in a common format.
Retention after that follows the Privacy Policy: personal and contact data deleted within 180 days,
financial and invoice records kept for 6 years as Indian tax law requires, then deleted.

## 10. Support

Support is by `[WhatsApp / email support@cerebyl.com]`, `[Mon–Sat, 10:00–19:00 IST]`. Data-protection
questions and grievances: Harish Sharma, Grievance Officer, support@cerebyl.com.

## 11. General

Governed by the laws of India; courts at `[Karnal, Haryana]` have exclusive jurisdiction. This
agreement, the Terms of Service and the DPA are the whole agreement for the pilot; if they conflict,
this agreement wins for the pilot period.
