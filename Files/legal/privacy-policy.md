# Privacy Policy — Cerebyl

**Last updated: 30 July 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how Cerebyl ("Cerebyl", "we", "us", "our") collects, uses,
stores, and protects information when you use our software application available at
https://app.cerebyl.com (the "Service"). Cerebyl is operated by Harish Sharma, a sole
proprietor based in Karnal, Haryana, India.

Cerebyl is a business tool. It is a customer-relationship and business-management application
for pharmaceutical franchise (PCD) businesses. **Cerebyl does not provide medical advice,
diagnosis, or treatment, and makes no medical claims of any kind.**

By using the Service you agree to this Policy. If you do not agree, do not use the Service.

## 1. Who is responsible for your data

The Service is used by businesses ("Customer Companies") that create accounts and invite their
own staff. Two roles matter under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act"):

- For the personal data of a Customer Company's own customers, parties, and contacts that the
  Customer Company enters into the Service, **the Customer Company is the Data Fiduciary** and
  **Cerebyl acts only as a Data Processor** on that Customer Company's instructions. Our handling
  of that data is governed by our Data Processing Agreement.
- For the account and login information of the Customer Company and its individual users (name,
  email, role), **Cerebyl is the Data Fiduciary**.

This Policy describes Cerebyl's own practices. Each Customer Company is responsible for its own
privacy notice to the individuals whose data it enters.

## 2. Information we collect

**Account information:** name, email address, phone number, assigned role, and company
association of each user, created when an account is set up.

**Business data you enter:** leads, parties/customers, contacts, orders, invoices, payments,
dues, products, stock, transporters, staff records, and related documents you upload. Some of
this may contain personal data of third parties (e.g. a customer's name and phone number).

**Usage and technical data:** log data, device and browser information, IP address, and actions
taken in the Service, collected to operate, secure, and improve it.

**AI assistant interactions:** when you use the in-app assistant, your questions and the
relevant business data needed to answer them are processed to generate a response.

We do **not** intentionally collect special-category or sensitive personal data, and you should
not enter health, biometric, financial-account, or government-ID numbers of individuals except
the ordinary business records the Service is designed to hold.

## 3. How we use information

We use information only to: provide and maintain the Service; authenticate users and enforce
access controls; generate the reports, invoices, and features you request; operate the AI
assistant; secure the Service and detect abuse; comply with law; and communicate with you about
your account. We do **not** sell your data. We do **not** use your business data or your
customers' data to train AI models.

## 4. Where your data is stored

Your data is stored on servers located in **India (Mumbai region)**. Cerebyl uses the following
processors to run the Service, some of which may process limited data outside India as part of
their global infrastructure:

- **Supabase** — database, authentication, and file storage (hosted in India, Mumbai).
- **Cloudflare** — application hosting, content delivery, and email routing.
- **Google (Gemini)** — powers the AI assistant feature.
- **Resend** — sending transactional email (e.g. notifications).

Each processor is engaged under terms requiring appropriate security and use of the data only
to provide their service to us. Where any transfer of personal data outside India occurs, it is
done only to the extent permitted by the DPDP Act and applicable law.

## 5. How long we keep data

We keep account and business data for as long as your account is active. After an account is
terminated, we delete personal and contact data within **180 days**, except that financial and
invoice records are retained for **6 years** to meet Indian statutory and tax obligations, after
which they are deleted. Backups are cycled on a rolling basis and purged in the ordinary course.

## 6. Your rights

Subject to the DPDP Act, you may request to: access the personal data we hold about you; correct
or update inaccurate data; and request deletion of your personal data. The Service also provides
in-app tools to delete records and to delete your account. To exercise a right, contact the
Grievance Officer below. We will respond within the timeframes required by law.

Where Cerebyl acts as a Processor for a Customer Company, requests from that company's customers
should be directed to the Customer Company, and we will assist that company in responding.

## 7. Security

We use access controls (row-level security isolating each company's data), encryption of
sensitive stored secrets, encrypted transport (HTTPS), authentication checks on every request,
and restricted administrative access. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure,
but we take reasonable measures to protect your data and to detect and address incidents.

## 8. Children

The Service is intended for business use and is **not** offered to, or directed at, individuals
under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect the personal data of children.

## 9. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. Material changes will be notified within the Service
or by email. The "Last updated" date above shows when it last changed.
