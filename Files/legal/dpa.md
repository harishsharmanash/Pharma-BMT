# Data Processing Agreement (DPA) — Cerebyl

**Last updated: 30 July 2026**

This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") forms part of the Terms of Service between the customer
business using Cerebyl ("Customer", the "Data Fiduciary") and Harish Sharma, sole proprietor,
operating Cerebyl ("Cerebyl", the "Data Processor"). It governs Cerebyl's processing of personal
data that the Customer enters into the Service on behalf of the Customer's own customers, parties,
and contacts ("Customer Personal Data"). It is written with reference to India's Digital Personal
Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act").

## 1. Roles

For Customer Personal Data, the **Customer is the Data Fiduciary** and determines the purposes of
processing; **Cerebyl is the Data Processor** and processes such data only on the Customer's
documented instructions, being the provision of the Service under the Terms.

## 2. Scope and purpose of processing

- **Subject matter:** provision of the Cerebyl business-management service.
- **Nature and purpose:** hosting, storage, retrieval, display, organisation, and processing of
  Customer Personal Data to deliver the Service's features (CRM, orders, invoicing, reporting,
  AI assistant, notifications).
- **Categories of data subjects:** the Customer's customers, parties, contacts, leads, and the
  Customer's own staff whose records the Customer enters.
- **Categories of data:** names, contact details, business addresses, order and transaction
  records, and other business data the Customer chooses to enter.

## 3. Cerebyl's obligations

Cerebyl will: (a) process Customer Personal Data only on the Customer's documented instructions
and as needed to provide the Service; (b) not sell Customer Personal Data and not use it to train
AI models; (c) ensure persons authorised to process the data are under confidentiality
obligations; (d) implement reasonable technical and organisational security measures (Section 5);
(e) assist the Customer, taking into account the nature of processing, in responding to data
principals exercising their rights and in meeting the Customer's security and breach obligations;
and (f) make available information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance with this DPA.

## 4. Sub-processors

The Customer authorises Cerebyl to engage the following sub-processors, each engaged under terms
requiring appropriate security and use of the data only to provide their service:

| Sub-processor | Purpose | Location |
|---|---|---|
| Supabase | Database, authentication, file storage | India (Mumbai) |
| Cloudflare | Hosting, content delivery, email routing | Global CDN |
| Google (Gemini) | AI assistant feature | Google infrastructure |
| Resend | Transactional email delivery | Provider infrastructure |

Cerebyl will give the Customer reasonable notice of any intended addition or replacement of a
sub-processor, giving the Customer the opportunity to object.

## 5. Security

Cerebyl maintains reasonable technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risk,
including: per-company data isolation enforced by database row-level security; authentication and
role-based access control on every request; encryption of sensitive stored secrets; encrypted
transport (HTTPS); restricted administrative access; and logging and monitoring to detect and
respond to incidents.

## 6. Personal data breach

Cerebyl will notify the Customer without undue delay after becoming aware of a personal data
breach affecting Customer Personal Data, and will provide information reasonably available to
help the Customer meet its own notification obligations under the DPDP Act.

## 7. Data location and transfers

Customer Personal Data is stored in India (Mumbai region). Any processing outside India by a
sub-processor is limited to what is necessary to provide the Service and is carried out only to
the extent permitted by the DPDP Act and applicable law.

## 8. Return and deletion

On termination of the Service, Cerebyl will delete personal and contact data within 180 days,
except financial and invoice records which are retained for 6 years to meet Indian statutory
obligations and then deleted. On the Customer's written request before deletion, Cerebyl will
make available an export of Customer Personal Data in a commonly used format where technically
feasible.

## 9. Liability

Each party's liability under this DPA is subject to the limitations of liability in the Terms of
Service.
